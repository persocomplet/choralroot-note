ChoralRoot 0.28-note1 is **0.28 with six Options for Ableton Note** on an iPhone or iPad: recording the FM-1 one part
at a time, on the beat and in key ([ABLETON-NOTE.md](../ABLETON-NOTE.md), in French). It is not an official
release: the web installer does not offer it, `tools/fm1_install.py` installs it. With the six Options at their
defaults the FM-1 sends, records and stores exactly what 0.28 does.

## Ableton Note (new)

Options, after Output:

- **Part to Note** (All, Chords, Bass, Raw Chord): which part goes out on MIDI, since Note puts everything it
  receives into the open track; the FM-1 still plays every part. **GLO held + BASS** steps All, Chords, Bass from
  anywhere but the sound editor, without opening anything (the bass is not switched).
- **Note Audio** (Master, Follow Part): ChoralRoot In's channels 1-2 carry the master, or the part Part to Note
  names, dry (its pair from channels 3-4 or 5-6: before the effects, at half level, in step with the master).
- **Note Oct Chords**, **Note Oct Bass** (-2 .. +2 octaves): the MIDI notes shifted for Note's instruments; a
  note-off and the polyphonic aftertouch go to the note as it went out, whatever the octave is set to meanwhile.
- **Note Start/Stop** (Off, ch 1 .. 16): CC 102, 127 then 0, when the loop starts or stops playing: mapped to Note's
  Start/Stop it starts Note on the FM-1's first beat. OCT+ on the entry sends it at once, for Note's MIDI mapping.
  Not sent by the loop with MIDI Clock In (a Link-to-clock bridge then drives the FM-1, and a press would stop it).
- **Note Setup** (OCT+): USB Record On, 44.1 + 48 kHz, USB Level Fixed, the parts' MIDI on, Part to Note Chords,
  Note Audio Follow Part, Note Start/Stop ch 16. The line under it says what Note's Key & Scale and tempo should be
  (`Note: D Minor · 96 BPM`: Key Mode's tonic with its Transpose, the scale, the tempo).

## What is stored

The five values ride in spare bits of four automatic bytes of the settings block (cr_settings.h "The Ableton Note
Options"): `midi_en[]` bits 1 .. 3 (the octaves), `raw_sound` bits 6 .. 7 (Part to Note), `usb_level` bit 1 (Note
Audio), `clock_mode` bits 2 .. 6 (Note Start/Stop). No version change, nothing to migrate: 0 is every default and every
older record has 0 there. They are stored by themselves as the other Options are; Reset Settings clears them. An older
firmware reads a byte with a Note bit set as out of range and takes that byte's default (MIDI Clock Out, USB Level
Master, Raw Chord Sound and MIDI Keys Off, the stream's MIDI out as by default).

## The Options list

The six entries come after Output, so every entry before them keeps its place; Version, Calibrate and Reset Settings
are six places further (the emulator's scripts and checks that count to them are moved with them).
